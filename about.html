What We Supply

Products & Services Overview

From the door slab to the hardware set to the electronic access system, we supply and install the complete commercial door opening package.

Commercial Steel Doors

Hollow metal doors built to ANSI/SDI standards. Available in flush, stile-and-rail, and vision lite configurations for interior and exterior applications.

Wood & Composite Doors

Architectural wood doors in solid core and hollow core profiles. Stain-grade, paint-grade, and laminate face options to match any interior design requirement.

Fire-Rated Door Assemblies

UL-listed fire door and frame assemblies rated from 20 minutes to 3 hours. Properly labeled, gasketed, and hardware-coordinated for code compliance.

Hollow Metal Frames

Knock-down and welded steel frames for masonry, drywall, and existing opening applications. Custom sizing and profiles available for retrofit and new construction.

Locksets & Cylinders

Mortise, cylindrical, and deadbolt locksets in commercial grades from storeroom to office function. Key systems, master key programs, and high-security cylinder options.

Door Closers & Hinges

Surface-mounted and concealed door closers, continuous hinges, butt hinges, and pivot sets for all opening frequencies and door weights.

Exit Devices & Panic Hardware

Rim, mortise, and surface vertical rod exit devices meeting UL10C and NFPA 101 life safety code requirements. Trim options and dogging features available.

Access Control Systems

Card reader, keypad, and biometric entry systems integrated with electric strikes, magnetic locks, and door position switches for complete electronic access management.

Automatic Door Operators

ADA-compliant low-energy operators for swing doors. Ideal for healthcare, retail, and office applications requiring accessible hands-free entry.

CCTV & Surveillance

HD IP camera systems with cloud and on-premise recording for commercial facilities. Integrated with access control for complete perimeter security.

Hotels & Multi-Family Packages

Coordinated door, frame, hardware, and lock packages purpose-built for hospitality and residential occupancies. RFID guest room systems and fire-rated corridor assemblies.

Budgeting & Estimating

Professional door and hardware takeoffs from architectural plans. Detailed bid documents, value engineering options, and project management from order through delivery.
